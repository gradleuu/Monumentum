### About Monumentum
<p align="justify">
  Monumentum is an <strong>unofficial</strong>, client-side Fabric mod built specifically for the <a href="https://www.playmonumenta.com/">Monumenta Complete-the-Monument MMORPG↗</a> server. It adds features made specifically for that server's gameplay. While feedback and feature suggestions are more than welcome, this project remains a personal tool at its core. It was originally developed solely for my own use before being made publicly available; consequently, development priorities, updates, and overall maintenance are driven *mostly by my own needs.</p>
<p align="justify">
If future Monumenta's rules update alters the legality of certain features, it remains your responsibility to disable those features or remove the mod until an update is published. Monumentum is <b>use-at-your-own-risk</b>. Even though its features are designed to stay within Monumenta's rules - some may or not fall into gray areas. Therefore I'm not responsible for any bans resulting from misuse. 

Monumentum is <b>not</b> affiliated with Team Monumenta - Monumentum is only tested and designed to work on their server, Monumenta.</p>
